## Calibration Questions

### Question 1
- **Content**: How many students applied for the University of Tartu programs offered in Estonian?
- **Answer**: 9690
- **Context**: 7,600 students applied for the 53 undergraduate programs and 2,090 for the 47 master's programs. A total of 3,915 places were on offer.
- **Source**: [TÜ](https://ut.ee/et/sisu/tartu-ulikooli-kandideerimine-loppenud-0)

### Question 2
- **Content**: How many socks were lost in Estonia in 2024?
- **Answer**: 1369285
- **Use Log Scoring**: true
- **Source**: [Statistika amet](https://www.instagram.com/p/DH5DbTCiXYb)

### Question 3
- **Content**: On August 29th, there were 785,272 egg-laying hens in Estonia. How many of them lived in cages?
- **Answer**: 598438
- **Context**: Hens are often kept in cages smaller than a sheet of A4 paper.
- **Use Log Scoring**: true
- **Source**: [PRIA](https://kls.pria.ee/kaart/)

### Question 4
- **Content**: How many pairs of skis were imported to Estonia in 2024?
- **Answer**: 41003
- **Use Log Scoring**: true
- **Source**: [Statistika amet](https://www.instagram.com/p/DGFZYx6JJq2)

### Question 5
- **Content**: What was the average monthly gross wage in Estonia's construction sector in 1994, adjusted for inflation to 2024?
- **Answer**: 701
- **Unit**: €
- **Context**: After the kroon replaced the ruble in 1992, inflation was 89.8% in 1993 and 47.6% in 1994. In 2011, the euro was introduced at the rate of 1€ per 15.6466 krooni.
- **Source**: [officialdata.org](https://www.officialdata.org/estonia/inflation/1994?endYear=2024&amount=131) & [Statistika amet](https://andmed.stat.ee/en/stat/Lepetatud_tabelid__Majandus.%20Arhiiv__Palk%20ja%20toojeukulu.%20Arhiiv/PA91/table/tableViewLayout2)

### Question 6
- **Content**: In 2024, Estonia harvested 1 303 500 tonnes of grains, with wheat as the largest crop. What percentage corresponds to the second-largest crop, barley?
- **Answer**: 24
- **Unit**: %
- **Context**: Wheat had 57%, oats 10% and rye 6%.
- **Source**: [Statistika amet](https://www.instagram.com/p/DFZhHWTp5bo)

### Question 7
- **Content**: In 2022, how many computed tomography (CT) scans were performed in Estonia?
- **Answer**: 201609
- **Use Log Scoring**: true
- **Source**: [Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/hlth_co_exam__custom_18150866/default/table)

### Question 8
- **Content**: What percentage of Estonian household waste was recycled in 2023?
- **Answer**: 38
- **Unit**: %
- **Context**: In 2024, the average Estonian resident generated 373kg of household waste. Poland and Romania were the only EU countries generating less.
- **Source**: [Statistika amet](https://www.instagram.com/p/DHVHKQ7qbWp)

### Question 10
- **Content**: Topographic prominence measures how much a peak rises above the highest point on the way to a higher peak. What is the prominence of Suur Munamägi, Estonia's highest point?
- **Answer**: 217m
- **Base Value**: 140m
- **Context**: Suur Munamägi, with an elevation of 317.2m, is also the highest peak in the Baltic nations.
- **Source**: [PeakVisor](https://peakvisor.com/peak/suur-munamaegi.html)

### Question 11
- **Content**: How much coffee was consumed in Estonia in 2023?
- **Answer**: 5705 t
- **Base Value**: 4100 t
- **Context**: Estonia imported the most of its non-roasted coffee from Brazil, roasted coffee from Finland, and decaffeinated from Lithuania and Latvia. 
- **Source**: [Statistika amet](https://www.instagram.com/p/DAh19CJJ4HM)
